# Search Management & Theory

This section contains planning tools, statistical models, and operational frameworks for SAR management teams and incident commanders.

---

## Subcategories & Focus Areas
- **Lost Person Behavior (LPB)**: Statistical categories, distance tables, and reflex tasking guides
- **Search Theory**: Probability of Area (POA), Probability of Detection (POD), and sweep width calculations
- **Initial Response Protocols**: Hasty searches, containment points, and attraction strategies
- **Incident Command System (ICS)**: Briefing sheets, IAP (Incident Action Plan) templates, debrief forms
