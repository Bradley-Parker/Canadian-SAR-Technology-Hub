## Avenza Maps Overview

Avenza Maps is a mobile app that allows you to view maps and navigate using your phone or tablet, even when you do not have cell service.

Unlike a regular PDF, Avenza can use GeoPDFs (geo-referenced PDFs). These maps know where you are on the ground and can show your position directly on the map.

With a geo-referenced PDF loaded into Avenza, you can:

- See your current location on the map
- Get coordinates for your location
- Measure distance to other points
- Find directions and bearings (azimuths)
- Record tracks and waypoints

Think of it as a very smart paper map.


## Why Use Avenza in SAR?

Most SAR teams use CalTopo for planning, mapping, and sharing information.

However, not every agency or partner organization has access to CalTopo or is familiar with using it.

Organizations such as:
- Natural Resources
- Fish & Wildlife
- ATV clubs
- Public safety agencies
- Community volunteers
- Other supporting organizations

may already use Avenza and know how to work with it.

In these situations, it can be faster and easier to provide a geo-referenced PDF from CalTopo instead of trying to get everyone into CalTopo.

This gives them a digital map they can immediately use on their own device.

## How Does It Work?

Every map made by CalTopo is already a GeoPDF.When someone opens that PDF in Avenza:

- The map is imported into Avenza.
- Avenza reads the location information stored in the PDF.
- The user's GPS position appears on the map.
- They can navigate and measure distances just like they would with other Avenza maps.

No cell service or WiFi is required after the map has been downloaded.

## Practical Tips for the Field

- Verify map coverage before deployment.
- Keep device battery warm in cold weather and consider using an external battery pack to keep your phone charged.
- Do not depend on a single navigation method. Have a paper map, compass, dedicated GPS unit, or other way to navigate besides a smartphone.
